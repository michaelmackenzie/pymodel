** Known small differences from Combine
- hfmodel: pyhf interpolation for asymmetric lnN inside |theta| < 0.5 and for template

- Combine rounds template kappas to %f (about 1e-6 relative)

  mis-normalises some RooGenericPdfs (3.4% on mumem_75 rmc_ext)
